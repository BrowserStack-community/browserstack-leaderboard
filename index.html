🌊 Ride the Tide Bonus Programme
🌊

Teamwork earns a 15% bonus for your entire chapter

If ALL active Chapter Leaders in a city achieve Tier 3 or higher by quarter-end, every leader in that chapter receives a 15% bonus added to their quarterly total. Encourages collaboration over internal competition.

This bonus is calculated by the DevRel team at quarter-end and applied before final payout. It is shown as a preview on the leaderboard where the condition is already met.

📊 Points Philosophy — Combined vs Individual
ℹ

Why split points?

Combined points prevent friction between co-leaders sharing a chapter. Individual points reward personal hustle independently of the chapter.

Combined Points — Awarded to all active Chapter Leaders who co-host or contribute to the chapter's meetup activity that quarter.

Individual Points — Points each Chapter Leader earns independently through personal promotion, referrals, product engagement, and community growth.

🛡 Guardrails & Operational Principles
  • 📅Quarterly Reset — Leaderboard totals, tier status, and point counters reset at the start of each quarter. Every Chapter Leader gets a fresh competitive start.
  • 🔍Verification & Audit — All activity form submissions and tracking links are audited by the DevRel team prior to final tier payouts.
  • 🚚Reward Distribution — Physical rewards, badges, and certificates are processed and dispatched within 30 days of the quarter-end review.
  • ✈Conference Sponsorship — If your talk proposal featuring a BrowserStack topic is accepted at a recognised tech conference, BrowserStack sponsors your travel and accommodation (Tier 1 only).
  • 🌊Ride the Tide — 15% bonus applied at quarter-end if ALL CLs in a city reach Tier 3 or higher. Calculated by DevRel team before final payouts.
  • 💬Discord Cap — Points are capped at 50 joins per channel (Chapter QR + Individual UTM = separate caps) to prioritise member quality over volume.
