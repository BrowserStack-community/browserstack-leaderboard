Welcome to the Chapter Leader Portal
Start with the 4-step guide below. It explains what you need to do, how points work, and how you move through the leaderboard tiers.
📘 Start here

How the Chapter Leader Leaderboard works

You earn points for hosting strong meetups and contributing to the BrowserStack community during the quarter. Some points are shared with your chapter co-hosts, while others are earned by you individually. Your total points determine the tier and rewards you unlock.

💡New Chapter Leader? Your first goal is simple: successfully host one primary meetup. That unlocks Builder status and your annual Chapter Leader swag kit.
1
Host your primary meetup
Successfully host one primary meetup during the quarter to become a Builder.
2
Earn points
Earn points through meetup execution, community contributions, referrals, Discord growth and other activities.
3
Submit what needs proof
Use the Activity Form for activities that are not automatically tracked. Other activities are tracked by the DevRel team.
4
Move up the tiers
Your points add up through the quarter. Reach 750, 1,550 or 2,250 points to unlock higher tiers and rewards.
🧩 First, understand your two point types

Your leaderboard total can include both Combined Points and Individual Points. The difference is who receives the points.

🔗 Combined Points

Points earned from activities that benefit the whole chapter. These points are credited to the active Chapter Leaders co-hosting that chapter.

Examples: hosting the meetup, securing the venue, finding a guest speaker, attendance performance and chapter Discord joins.
⚡ Individual Points

Points earned personally for your own contribution. These points are credited only to the Chapter Leader who completed the activity.

Examples: speaking about BrowserStack, attending CL Connect, referrals, meetup highlights, alpha activities and your personal Discord UTM joins.
🧮
Your leaderboard score = Combined Points + Individual Points
Your Dashboard shows the split so you can see exactly where your points are coming from.
🏆 Your tier journey

Points accumulate during the quarter. You keep progressing as your total increases; you do not need to choose a tier.

✨ Three special rules worth knowing

These can make a meaningful difference to your score or benefits, so keep them in mind throughout the quarter.

🌊
Ride the Tide: +15%
If all active Chapter Leaders in your city reach Tier 3 or higher, every leader in that chapter receives a 15% bonus point boost. Help your co-hosts progress too.
✈️
Conference Sponsorship
If your talk proposal featuring a BrowserStack topic is accepted at a recognised conference, BrowserStack will sponsor travel and accommodation. This is funded separately from tier rewards.
💬
Discord: 10 pts per join
Each verified new member earns 10 points. Chapter QR joins and personal UTM joins are tracked separately, with a 50-join cap per source channel.

🛡️ Important rules
📅
Points reset every quarter
Leaderboard totals, tier status and point counters start fresh at the beginning of each quarter.
🔎
Points are verified
Activity submissions and tracked links are audited by the DevRel team before final tier rewards are confirmed.
🚚
Rewards follow quarter-end review
Physical rewards, badges and certificates are processed within 30 days of the quarter-end review.
❓ Common questions
Do my points carry into the next quarter?

No. Leaderboard totals, tier status and point counters reset at the start of every quarter.

Do all Chapter Leaders get the same meetup points?

Combined activities are credited to the active Chapter Leaders co-hosting the chapter. Individual activities are credited only to the person who completed them.

Where do I submit an activity?

Use the Activity Form in this portal whenever the points reference says “Activity Form.” Automatically tracked activities do not require a separate submission.

How do Discord points work?

Each verified new member is worth 10 points. Chapter QR joins and personal UTM joins are tracked as separate source channels, each capped at 50 joins.

What is Ride the Tide?

If every active Chapter Leader in a city reaches Tier 3 or higher, each leader in that chapter receives a 15% bonus point boost.
