🌟 Key Programme Initiatives
🌊
Ride the Tide
If all active Chapter Leaders in a city reach Tier 3 or higher, every leader in that chapter gets a +15% bonus point boost. A chapter wins together.
✈
Conference Travel & Stay Sponsorship
If a CL's talk proposal featuring a BrowserStack topic gets accepted at a recognised conference, BrowserStack will sponsor travel and accommodation. Funded separately from tier rewards.

Discord Point Policy
Joins earn 10 points per verified member via Chapter QR or Individual UTM links. Capped at 50 joins per source channel to keep rankings balanced.

🛡 Guardrails
  • 📅Quarterly Reset — Leaderboard totals, tier status, and point counters reset at the beginning of each quarter.
  • 🔍Verification & Audit — All activity form submissions and tracking links are audited by the DevRel team prior to final tier payouts.
  • 🚚Reward Distribution — Physical rewards, badges, and certificates are processed and dispatched within 30 days of the quarter-end review.
